Delete your Sunsprout account

You can delete your Sunsprout account and its data in two ways.

From the game (immediately)

  1. Open Sunsprout and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the game (by email)

Email midwayofdraenor@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Sunsprout account" from the address you signed in with (Apple, Google or Discord). If you played as a guest, tell us your player name as it appears on the leaderboard. We delete it within 30 days at most and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • The account and its link to Apple, Google or Discord (for Apple, access is also revoked).
  • The cloud copy of your progress.
  • Your leaderboard entries and usage statistics.

What is kept

  • Purchase records required by law (up to 6 years in Spain), with no data that identifies you beyond the transaction identifier. Purchases stay with your Apple or Google account and come back with "Restore purchases".
  • The progress saved on your device, until you erase it with "Reset progress" or uninstall the game.

More information in the privacy policy.
